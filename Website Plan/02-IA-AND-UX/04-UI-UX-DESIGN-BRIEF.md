# UI/UX Design Brief

- Project: Modern Guideways
- Version: 2.0
- Status: Planning specification / owner review
- Updated: 2026-11-06

## Project context and working assumptions
- Company: Modern Guideways.
- Focus: machine protection systems for CNC/VMC/HMC equipment.
- Product categories: telescopic covers, roller covers, apron covers, bellows, guide wipers and enclosing guards.
- Visual direction: Metallic Blue, deep navy, graphite, steel grey and clean light surfaces.
- Typography direction used in previous iterations: Outfit display + Plus Jakarta Sans body; validate font files/licensing.
- Existing project has been described as having 1,101 Full HD WebP frames; verify actual assets and manifest before making implementation claims.
- Contact/address details previously supplied must be owner-confirmed before public release.
- No ecommerce, payment gateway or unnecessary backend is assumed.
- The initial hosting cost constraint is low-cost/static; re-check provider terms at deployment time.

Define visual direction and experience principles.

## Brand direction
Premium industrial engineering: precise, confident, practical, modern and manufacturing-focused. Use Metallic Blue sampled from the approved logo, deep navy, graphite, steel grey and light neutral surfaces.

## Experience principles
- Large editorial typography with compact technical labels.
- Real product photography/CAD renders rather than generic stock.
- Clear hierarchy and clear enquiry calls to action.
- White space and purposeful composition.
- Motion should explain product behavior, guide attention or provide feedback.
- No repetitive card-grid pattern across every section.

## Section direction
- Overview: cinematic machine hero, clear headline, value proposition and path to products.
- Products: six-scene horizontal showcase with unique product reveals.
- Gallery: image-led editorial grid with staggered entrance and accessible detail view.
- About Us: company story, approved facts and a drawn process/timeline.
- Contact: concise contact rows, location details and direct action links.

## Design exclusions
Avoid random gradients, heavy glassmorphism, fake statistics, fake customer logos, fabricated certifications, overused pills, gratuitous parallax and decorative UI that competes with the machinery.

## Completion evidence
Record the actual source files, commands, browser/device, screenshots or video evidence, test results and owner approval associated with this document. A plan or successful compilation is not proof of runtime behavior.
