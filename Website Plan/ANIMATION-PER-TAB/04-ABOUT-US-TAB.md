# Tab Animation 04 — About Us

## Signature motion: Engineering timeline draw
**Trend-informed pattern:** SVG path drawing + sequential content reveal + editorial storytelling.

### Entry sequence
- Main heading reveals line-by-line.
- A thin technical timeline/connector draws across the section.
- Milestone blocks enter in sequence, tied to viewport visibility.
- Company/leadership image enters with a subtle clipped mask.

### Content
Use only confirmed legal business name, leadership, history, experience statement, capabilities and location. All milestone dates and years require owner evidence.

### Motion rules
- Timeline must remain understandable if path drawing is disabled.
- Do not invent company age or historical milestones.
- No auto-scrolling or forced snap between milestones.

### Acceptance
Logical reading order remains intact; headings and facts are accessible; timeline does not hide text; reduced-motion users see the static complete timeline.
