# Product Scene — Apron Covers

## Unique transition
**Slat-by-slat cascade.**

Reveal metal slats in a fast, ordered cascade from left to right, then show the whole articulated shape. Use subtle stagger rather than flashing or aggressive 3D flips.

## Content and imagery
Use approved apron-cover imagery and verify material/build.

## Accessibility and responsive behavior
On reduced motion, show the complete product image and content immediately. On mobile, use a simple swipe/snap scene and visible controls; do not force pinning.

## QA checks
- Product image matches real approved product.
- Product title and index are correct.
- Transition can be reversed and interrupted.
- Text remains readable and CTA reachable.
- No false performance or material claims.
