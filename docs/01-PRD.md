# 01 — Product Requirements Document

**Project:** Modern Guideways Website  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Starter — owner review required  
**Last updated:** 2026-11-07  
**Owner:** TBD

## 1. Purpose
The website introduces Modern Guideways and converts interested industrial visitors into direct enquiries.

## 2. Project context
Visitors must understand the company machine-cover categories, service context and contact routes.

## 3. Requirements and implementation
Static responsive site, cinematic scroll story, product categories, company story and contact links.

## 4. Scope boundaries and risks
No checkout, accounts, admin panel, CMS, database or analytics in v1.

## 5. Acceptance criteria
All primary sections render; mobile navigation works; every contact link opens the intended destination; scroll story responds in both directions.

## 6. Dependencies and open items
- Confirm company-approved brand assets, business facts and content before public launch.
- Refer to [Project Brief](../PROJECT-BRIEF.md), [Decision Register](../DECISION-REGISTER.md) and [Document Index](../DOC-INDEX.md).
- The generated site is a starter implementation, not evidence of a completed production release.
