## 1. Purpose
The static website itself does not collect or store form submissions. Contact links hand off to the the visitor chosen app.

## 2. Project context

## 3. Requirements and implementation
Add a reviewed privacy notice if analytics, cookies, server-side forms or embedded third-party widgets are introduced.

## 4. Scope boundaries and risks
Legal review and final privacy/terms text are owner responsibilities.

## 5. Acceptance criteria
Publish only reviewed legal copy; document any later personal-data collection.
