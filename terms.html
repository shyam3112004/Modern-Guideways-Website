1. Scope of Operations & Services

Modern Guideways specializes in the design, custom fabrication, retrofitting, and reconditioning of machine tool protection systems, including but not limited to:

  • Telescopic steel way covers (heavy-gauge cold-rolled and stainless steel).
  • Industrial roll-up guards and spring-loaded roller covers.
  • Aluminum link apron covers and armor-clad bellows.
  • Fabric and polyurethane guide bellows with PVC reinforcement ribs.
  • Synthetic polyurethane lip wipers and brass chip deflectors.
  • Complete CNC/VMC/HMC machine safety enclosures and sheet metal guards.

Clients are responsible for confirming physical travel strokes, clearances, bracket hole pitch, and maximum rapid traverse speeds of their host machine tool.
