02 / PRECISION PRODUCTS PHASE 01 / SIX ENGINEERED SYSTEMS

ENGINEERED MACHINE PROTECTION

Six core machine protection systems engineered to converge into a fully protected CNC machining center.

6 SYSTEMS · 1 CNC ASSEMBLY
01 / WAY DEFENSE SPEED 60m/min · 1.5–3mm STEEL

Telescopic Covers

Heavy-duty multi-section box steel covers with brass gliders and polyurethane wipers, safeguarding CNC, VMC and HMC machine bedways against sharp hot chips, heavy coolant washdown and mechanical collision.

02 / RAPID APERTURE SPEED 90m/min · CONSTANT TENSION

Roller Covers

High-velocity spring-tensioned roll-up curtains engineered with stainless steel bands or high-strength coated fabric for space-restricted machine axes, rapid cycle traverse, and coolant protection.

03 / SLAT DEFENSE ALU-LINK · ARTICULATED SLATS

Apron Covers

Flexible interlocking anodized aluminum slats with high-durability polyurethane hinge joints, providing rugged deflection against heavy chip bombardment, deep drop-offs, and roll-up way protection.

04 / EXPANSION SHIELD STROKE 1:10 · 120m/min

Bellows

Precision accordion way bellows crafted from multi-ply coated fabric with internal PVC stiffener guides and optional stainless steel lamella scales, sealing laser, grinding, and multi-axis CNC slideways.

05 / WAY WIPERS 85 SHORE A · PRELOADED LIP

Lip Wipers

Precision-molded polyurethane and NBR elastomer guide wipers with rigid metal backings, continuously clearing microscopic fines, abrasive swarf, and fluid film to prevent guideway scoring.

06 / PERIMETER ARMOR ISO 23125 · CRCA STEEL

Enclosing Guard

Complete modular machine tool safety cabins featuring heavy-gauge CRCA sheet steel panels, impact-resistant laminated polycarbonate viewing windows, safety interlocks, and 100% fluid mist containment.
