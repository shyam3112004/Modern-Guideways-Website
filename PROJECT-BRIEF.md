Create a distinctive, premium industrial website that explains Modern Guideways' machine-cover service and manufacturing categories and encourages genuine B2B enquiries.

## Audience
Industrial buyers, machine tool owners, maintenance teams, production engineers, procurement teams and service partners.

## Website outcome
Visitors should quickly understand the product categories, view the supplied manufacturing story, and contact the company to discuss a machine-specific requirement.

## Scope v1
- Responsive static website.
- Scroll-driven image sequence from the supplied MP4.
- Company introduction, products, process/capability messaging, visual story and contact.
- Click-to-call, email, WhatsApp and directions.
- Basic metadata and semantic HTML.

## Excluded from v1
- E-commerce, pricing catalogue, user accounts, admin dashboard, CMS, database, CRM integration, analytics tracking, payment processing and automated email delivery.

## Design direction
Deep navy, steel grey, restrained indigo/blue, white and editorial typography. Industrial precision, generous negative space, strong grid, purposeful motion and no generic AI-template patterns.
