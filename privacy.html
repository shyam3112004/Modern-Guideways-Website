COMPLIANCE & INDUSTRIAL PRIVACY STANDARD

Privacy Policy

EFFECTIVE DATE: NOVEMBER 10, 2026 • JURISDICTION: DHARMAPURI, TAMIL NADU, INDIA

1. Introduction & Corporate Scope

Modern Guideways ("we", "our", or "us"), situated at 2/186, Gollapatti (Vill), Selliam Patti (Po), Palacode (Tk), Dharmapuri (Dt), Tamil Nadu – 636 809, is an engineered machine protection manufacturing and service enterprise. We respect the confidentiality of all industrial engineering drawings, machine travel specifications, and client personnel information entrusted to us.

This Privacy Policy outlines our procedures concerning the collection, storage, and processing of technical enquiry data across our digital platforms and communication channels.

2. Information We Collect

When you interact with our website or submit an engineering requirement, we may gather:

  • Contact Information: Full name, company/plant name, corporate email address, and direct telephone/WhatsApp number.
  • Machine & Technical Specifications: Machine brand/model, axis travel dimensions (X, Y, Z stroke), clearance profiles, operating speeds, coolant exposure, and custom sheet metal notes.
  • Technical Documentation: Uploaded CAD files, engineering blueprints, damage photographs, or dimensional sketches submitted via our quotation desk.
  • Digital Telemetry: Anonymized server logs, browser user agents, and IP addresses for connection security and system stability.

3. Purpose of Processing

The information captured is utilized exclusively for genuine B2B industrial operations:

  • Engineering feasibility assessment and quotation preparation for telescopic covers, roll-up guards, bellows, and custom wipers.
  • Direct technical correspondence with plant maintenance managers and procurement personnel via WhatsApp, phone, or email.
  • Manufacturing logistics, on-site measurement scheduling, and dispatch coordination.
  • Administrative record-keeping and statutory tax compliance under Indian commercial law.

4. Non-Disclosure of Proprietary Machine Drawings

All machine blueprints, CAD designs, and manufacturing tolerances submitted by clients are treated as proprietary commercial secrets. Modern Guideways does not sell, rent, or lease engineering drawings to third parties or competitor facilities under any circumstances.

5. Cookies & Local Storage

Our website utilizes local storage and lightweight cookies solely to maintain state synchronization (such as preloading animation frame indices and caching customer enquiry drafts). We do not deploy invasive third-party cross-site advertising trackers.

6. Data Security & Storage

We deploy standard encryption protocols (HTTPS/TLS) across all digital transmissions. Administrative access to enquiry logs and client databases is restricted to authorized Modern Guideways engineering personnel via credential-gated portals.

7. Statutory Compliance & User Rights

In accordance with the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023, you retain the right to review, update, or request the deletion of your corporate contact data from our records at any time.
