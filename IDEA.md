# Modern Guideways — Premium Industrial Website Development

Build a world-class, premium industrial manufacturing website for **MODERN GUIDEWAYS — MACHINE PROTECTION SYSTEMS**.

The goal is to create a visually distinctive, modern, cinematic and highly interactive website with professional UI/UX, advanced animations and strong industrial brand identity.

## 1. Brand Identity
- Preserve the original Modern Guideways MG logo.
- Metallic Blue, Deep Navy, Graphite and Steel Grey colour palette.
- Premium industrial visual direction.
- Outfit display typography and Plus Jakarta Sans body typography.
- Clean layouts, strong typography and professional visual hierarchy.
- Avoid generic AI-generated website designs and repetitive card layouts.

## 2. Five Individually Animated Navigation Tabs

Each main navigation tab must have its own distinctive, trending animation.

**01 — OVERVIEW**
- Cinematic machinery hero animation.
- Scroll-driven storytelling.
- Kinetic typography.
- Layered image reveals.
- Premium introduction transitions.

**02 — PRODUCTS**
- Full-width horizontal product storytelling.
- Zipper-style opening animation.
- Reveal one product at a time.
- Unique mechanical animation for every product.
- Smooth scroll synchronization and reverse animations.

**03 — GALLERY**
- Editorial image reveals.
- Staggered image transitions.
- Interactive lightbox.
- Smooth image masking and hover effects.

**04 — ABOUT US**
- Animated engineering timeline.
- Sequential content reveals.
- Premium company storytelling.
- Image and typography transitions.

**05 — CONTACT**
- Animated contact information.
- Interactive phone and email links.
- WhatsApp enquiry integration.
- Animated location and contact details.

## 3. Six Unique Product Animations

1. Telescopic Covers — mechanical sliding and nested-section opening.
2. Roller Covers — rolling horizontal reveal.
3. Apron Covers — individual metal-slat cascade.
4. Bellows — accordion-style unfolding.
5. Guide Wipers — precision horizontal wipe.
6. Enclosing Guards — mechanical frame and panel assembly.
